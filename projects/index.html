Workstreams

In development

Four threads run in parallel underneath the shipped work. Each has a named lead and is where new projects come from.

In development

Urban Digital Twin Platform

Scalable, efficient infrastructure for visualisation, analytics and simulation — the substrate every other workstream builds on.

In development

Spatial AI and Reasoning

Machine learning and data analytics for high-resolution spatial understanding: flow prediction, anomaly detection, and inference of the patterns cities leave in their data.

In development

Agentic Geospatial Intelligence

Agentic tools, servers and knowledge infrastructure that let frontier models reason about places rather than just describe them.

In development

Policy & Deployment

Translating research into practice through municipal partnerships, pilot digital-twin deployments, and honest assessment of what changed in the planning decisions that followed.
